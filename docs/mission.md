# Introduction & Company Mission

## Introduction

Welcome to **Niplex** — the open-source home of everything we build. Niplex is
a technology company rooted in AI, Machine Learning, and code. This
documentation site is the public face of that work: the projects, the
architecture, and the mission that drives it all.

## Company Mission

At Niplex, our mission is clear:

> **"Never stop Imagining."**

In an era where technology is advancing exponentially, we believe that all a
human needs to do is think, imagine, and strategize. The rest of the heavy
lifting — processing data, managing files, automating workflows, and executing
repetitive tasks — can be left to Artificial Intelligence (AI).

As a technology, AI, Machine Learning (ML), and code-based company, Niplex is
dedicated to empowering **non-technical users**. We help individuals understand
AI and teach them how to leverage modern tools like **Agentic Frameworks** and
**Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors**.

Our goal is to shift the burden of "heavy thinking" and "overthinking" to our
systems, allowing businesses to focus on what truly requires human ingenuity —
while AI handles tasks that take mere minutes to automate but hours to do
manually.
