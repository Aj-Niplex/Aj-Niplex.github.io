The Neural Stack

Memory as a product — honest answers in, routed knowledge out, capture everywhere

What It Is

Neural is the ecosystem's memory product line: Neural-MCP (a memory sub-agent that answers from the real knowledge store), neural-chat (a Discord + Telegram capture bot that files life into sections), and Neural-OS (a second-brain workspace app). Three systems, one idea: durable knowledge should be routed, honest, and human-readable — not dumped into one pile.

Neural-MCP — the honest memory sub-agent

Two tools define the interface: ask_neural and log_to_neural. The design rule that makes it different: Neural says "I don't know" when it doesn't know — it never confabulates from thin context. It runs on Agnes (agnes-2.0-flash) over the Adarshs-Stack knowledge store, with two auth layers in front.

The heart of agent.py is a real routing schema — messages are not dumped into a generic store, they are filed to the right Markdown path:

RouteWhat lands there
wiki/pages/niplex.mdNiPlex product knowledge
wiki/pages/kaizenmanas.mdThe Kaizen Manas agent-team system
decisions.mdDecision records with reasoning
academics-class-12.mdClass 12 study material and progress
japanese-learning.mdJLPT study log and vocabulary
lifestyle-goals.mdPersonal goals and habits
wiki/inbox.mdCatch-all for everything unclassified
Projects/Claude/logs/{today}.mdDaily agent logs
planning/{slug}.mdPer-plan planning documents
USER.mdOwner profile, merged and kept under 100 lines

A PRIORITY_PATHS list decides what the agent reads first, and server.py exposes it all as a FastMCP service on port 7861, behind a fail-secure Bearer middleware — no token configured, no server answering.

neural-chat — capture from anywhere

A Discord + Telegram bot with two modes: capture (file messages into knowledge) and chat. Its /setup command is idempotent — run it twice, nothing breaks. On Telegram, capture uses forum Topics and requires the Manage Topics permission; on Discord it creates the sections it needs.

The classifier prompt is fed by real section descriptions, so routing decisions are explainable:

SectionWhat it collects
unsorted 📥Catch-all while the classifier decides
projects 💼Builds, repos, work in flight
academics 🎓Study notes, exams, concepts
goals-and-plans 🎯Targets and the plan to reach them
decisions 🤔Choices made and why
ideas 💡Raw sparks worth keeping
daily-logDay-by-day record
personalEverything else personal

Neural-OS — the second brain

The workspace layer: a second-brain app built on React 19 + Convex + Tailwind, turning the captured knowledge into something browsable and queryable — the "Graphene Daily" project. Where Neural-MCP is the memory's API and neural-chat is its intake, Neural-OS is its face.
